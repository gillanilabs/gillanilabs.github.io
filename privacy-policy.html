Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps published by Gillani Labs ("we", "us") on Google Play (each an "App"). Gillani Labs is an independent developer based in Pakistan.

Our general approach: we do not require an account, we do not ask for your name, and information you enter in our Apps is stored on your device. Some Apps use Google services for ads and crash reporting, which collect limited device data as described below. Section 9 lists exactly what each App stores and which services it uses.

1. Information you enter in an App

Content and settings you create in our Apps are stored only on your device unless the App's section below says otherwise. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

  • If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by your Google account, not by us.
  • You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

2. Third-party services

Depending on the App (see Section 9), we use the following services provided by Google:

Google AdMob (advertising)

Apps that show ads use Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

If an App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash details such as the error trace, device model, Android version, App version and an installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix problems. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. What we do not collect

Unless an App's section below says otherwise:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. How information is used and shared

Information collected through the services above is used to show ads, keep our Apps free, and fix crashes. It is shared only with Google as the provider of these services, or when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google services is kept according to Google's own retention policies. For privacy questions or help with a deletion request, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

Data on your device is protected by Android's built-in app sandboxing, and data sent to Google services is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we release new Apps or change existing ones. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date shown at the top.
